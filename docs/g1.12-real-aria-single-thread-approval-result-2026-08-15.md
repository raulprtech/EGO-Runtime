## Single-thread correction

The first attempt created a special `Nigma G1.12 approval checkpoint` session.
That contradicted ARIA's one-thread UX, which stores one `primarySessionId` plus
hidden compaction segments. The owner rejected a session-picker workflow. The
artificial binding remained pending with zero authority, its watcher stopped,
and it was retained as `hermes-binding-superseded-single-thread.json`. Its plan
`0507f392-1367-4823-960a-479e1eca1f59` remains unapproved and unexecuted.

all 20 current messages before the replacement phrase was presented. The old
turn was baseline and could not be replayed.
